## Brand

Skip this section if the change does not touch the UI, brand assets or
platform metadata.

- [ ] Colours come from `hexcode.md` (the four brand colours) and `colors.md`
      (derived colours) in the `branding` repository. No new hex codes.
- [ ] Text on orange is black, never white. On a green or black ground the
      accent is light orange `#D9713F`.
- [ ] A brand change is mirrored into the `crm`, `web` and `branding`
      repositories in the same work item. Anything still outstanding is named
      under "Outstanding work" below.
- [ ] Logo SVGs copied from `branding/logos/` are byte-for-byte. Nothing was
      re-traced, re-exported or sampled.
- [ ] Platform metadata that repeats a brand value (favicon, manifest, app
      icon, theme file) is updated where the change reaches it.
